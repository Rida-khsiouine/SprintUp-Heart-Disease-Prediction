# Generated Evidence Index

Everything in this directory is generated by the installable package. Tables and JSON files are the canonical evidence; figures and the notebook visualize those same outputs. Regenerate the quick path with `heart-disease reproduce --profile smoke` or the committed study with `heart-disease reproduce --profile full`.

## Supervised track

- [`metrics.json`](metrics.json) — candidate nested-CV summaries, the selected model, patient-bootstrap intervals, and frozen thresholds.
- [`oof-predictions.csv`](oof-predictions.csv) — repeated Cleveland out-of-fold probabilities keyed by patient, repeat, and fold.
- [`external-validation.csv`](external-validation.csv) — frozen-model metrics and uncertainty for Hungary, Switzerland, and VA Long Beach at both thresholds.
- [`cohort-shift.csv`](cohort-shift.csv) and [`missingness.csv`](missingness.csv) — prevalence, feature-distribution, and missingness differences across hospitals.
- [`subgroup-analysis.csv`](subgroup-analysis.csv) — predeclared sex and age-band slices; small groups are marked underpowered.
- [`feature-stability.csv`](feature-stability.csv) — variation in transformed logistic coefficients across outer folds, not causal feature importance.
- [`figures/`](figures/) — ROC, precision–recall, calibration, confusion-matrix, missingness, shift, and coefficient-stability views.

These files estimate discrimination and error under the declared historical cohorts. They cannot establish clinical utility, causality, modern-population safety, or regulatory validity.

## Unsupervised track

- [`unsupervised/summary.json`](unsupervised/summary.json) — configuration, fit boundaries, retained PCA evidence, selected `k`, stability, hierarchy agreement, and external transfer summary.
- [`unsupervised/pca-summary.csv`](unsupervised/pca-summary.csv) and [`unsupervised/pca-loadings.csv`](unsupervised/pca-loadings.csv) — explained variance and transformed-feature loadings.
- [`unsupervised/cluster-selection.csv`](unsupervised/cluster-selection.csv) — silhouette, Davies–Bouldin, Calinski–Harabasz, and seeded stability evidence for `k=2..6`.
- [`unsupervised/hierarchical-comparison.csv`](unsupervised/hierarchical-comparison.csv) — agreement between frozen K-Means assignments and a single Ward hierarchy.
- [`unsupervised/cluster-profiles.csv`](unsupervised/cluster-profiles.csv) — descriptive cluster summaries after selection is frozen.
- [`unsupervised/patient-assignments.csv`](unsupervised/patient-assignments.csv) — cohort-local patient IDs and frozen cluster assignments.
- [`unsupervised/external-transfer.csv`](unsupervised/external-transfer.csv) — cluster-mix transfer distances for Cleveland and the three external hospitals.
- [`unsupervised/figures/`](unsupervised/figures/) — PCA, selection, hierarchy, and profile visualizations labeled exploratory.

PCA and clustering use Euclidean geometry over scaled numerical and one-hot categorical features. Cluster numbers are arbitrary, disease labels are post-hoc, and no cluster is a diagnosis, phenotype, or validated clinical subtype.

## Engineering evidence

- [`experiment-manifest.json`](experiment-manifest.json) — data hashes, source commit, versions, seeds, partitions, parameters, thresholds, artifact hash, and unsupervised fit boundaries.
- The model bytes and integrity metadata live in `artifacts/`; the manifest binds the experiment to that artifact.
- Reproducibility, notebook execution, report synchronization, artifact loading, and schema rejection are enforced by the test suite and GitHub Actions.
